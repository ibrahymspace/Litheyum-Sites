    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Does BIGGFISH post for me?

    Not yet. BIGGFISH takes a post all the way to approved and scheduled, then you publish it from the platform or your scheduler. Direct publishing is on the roadmap.

    Will it make things up?

    It is told never to invent numbers, dates, quotes or partnerships. When a fact is missing it writes [FACT NEEDED: what] in the draft and keeps going, so you know exactly what to check. The Brand Manager also scores every claim against your source.

    Can I use my own voice rules?

    Yes. The Voice & Brands room holds your house rules plus a profile for each brand with its audience, tone and banned words. Change a rule once and the whole team follows it on the next run.

    Which platforms does it write for?

    Captions are written natively for LinkedIn, Instagram and X, each with its own length limits and hashtag caps. The Ads Playbook walks you through publishing paid campaigns on Meta, LinkedIn, X and TikTok.

    Can I bring my existing content calendar?

    Yes. Import a CSV, an Excel sheet, a calendar file or JSON. BIGGFISH reads month grids too, the kind most teams keep in a spreadsheet.

    Which AI does it use?

    BIGGFISH runs on Claude by Anthropic. Each teammate is Claude working from a detailed brief you can open and edit in the Personas room.
